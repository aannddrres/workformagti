# Magti Internal Information Portal - Developer Specification (2026)

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

- **Current State:** Information is scattered across various files, chats, and emails.
- **Impact:** Operators waste time searching, leading to increased service time.
- **Management Need:** Visible statistics on mandatory updates.

## 2. Project Goals & Target Audience

- **Goal:** Centralize information, enable rapid search.
- **Target Audience:** Operators, Tech Support, Managers, Content Admins.

## 3. Unified Portal Modules

1. **Knowledge Base:** Articles, categories, search.
2. **Required Reading:** Status tracking (Read/Unread).
3. **Video Instructions:** Training materials.
4. **News & Notifications:** Alerts on updates.
5. **Admin Panel:** Content & user management.

## 4. User Roles & Permissions

- **Operator:** Search, read, favorites.
- **Manager:** Team statistics & reports.
- **Content Admin:** Create/Edit articles & videos.
- **System Admin:** Roles, security, audit logs.

## 5. Functional Requirements

- **Auth:** Internal email or SSO.
- **Search:** Global search by tags/title/body.
- **Required Reading:** Statuses: To Read / Read / Overdue.
- **Responsive:** Optimized for Desktop and Tablet.

## 6. Technical Requirements

- **Speed:** 1-2 seconds response time.
- **Security:** HTTPS, RBAC, SSO integration.
- **Scalability:** Ready for AI search/chatbot integration.
